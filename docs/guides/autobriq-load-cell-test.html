Testing the S-type load cell with the HX711

The load cell's 4 wires go to an HX711 amplifier board, and the HX711 goes to the ESP32 with 4 more. The actuator-test sketch from commit 9d4ac47 or later reads it: a LOAD box on the Nextion, kg and N on the phone page and in the serial monitor once a second.

The load cell's signal is tiny

A few millivolts at full load. It cannot go straight to the ESP32: the HX711 is required. Keep these wires short, twisted together, and away from the 12 V actuator and motor wires.

Before power

Meter check: find the wire pairs

Meter on ohms, load cell disconnected. A typical 350 Ω cell reads:

BetweenExpectMeans
Red and blackabout 380 to 420 ΩExcitation pair (E+ / E−), the highest reading
Green and whiteabout 350 ΩSignal pair (A+ / A−)
Any red or black to any green or whiteabout 280 to 300 ΩNormal: one leg of the bridge
Any pair open (OL) or near 0 ΩBroken wire or damaged cell
Test

Run it

  1. Flash and open the serial monitor

    pio run -e actuator-test -t upload
pio device monitor
    Leave the load cell unloaded while the ESP32 boots. About 3 s after power-up it zeroes itself and serial prints LOAD tared at raw .... The first readings before that jump around; ignore them.

  2. Check it's found

    Pass: LOAD: HX711 found, then once a second LOAD raw 123456 0.00 kg 0 N. The Nextion's top-right box shows LOAD 0.00 kg. Seeing LOAD no HX711 every few seconds means DT, SCK, VCC or GND isn't connected.

  3. Load it

    • Press on it through its end holes, or hang a weight from it, or stand something heavy on it.
    Pass: kg and N rise with the load and come back near 0 when you let go. Small jitter, about ±0.05 kg, is normal. On this rig pressing read negative, so the sketch has LOAD_SIGN = -1, which makes pressing positive. If yours still goes the wrong way, set LOAD_SIGN to 1 (or swap white and green at A− / A+). Not back at 0 after unloading: press t in the serial monitor to re-zero.

  4. Calibrate with a known weight

    • Unloaded, press t. Then put on a known weight, for example a 5 kg bag of rice, and note the raw number and the tare raw that t printed.
    LSB_PER_KG = |raw with weight − tare raw| ÷ weight in kg
    Put the result in LSB_PER_KG near the top of actuator_test.cpp and reflash. The README's 22,480 was measured at 5 V; at 3.3 V expect roughly two-thirds of that (about 14,800), so until you calibrate, readings show low.

  5. Press stops at the load limit

    • The 40 cm press stops by itself when LOAD reaches PRESS_STOP_KG (10 kg in the test build). In AUTO RUN that step ends and the run goes on to press HOME. HOME is never blocked.
    • Tap 40cm PRESS (R3) with the press head loaded against the cell and keep a hand on the E-stop.
    Pass: LOAD climbs positive, the press stops at the limit and serial prints LOAD 10.xx kg >= 10.0 kg: press stopped. Calibrate first, or the real force is about 50% higher than the screen shows. For real briquettes set PRESS_STOP_KG to your target (2000 N is about 204 kg; check that the actuator and the load cell are both rated above it).
Troubleshooting

What the reading tells you

You seeLikely cause
LOAD no HX711DT and SCK swapped, or VCC/GND missing. DT goes to GPIO19, SCK to GPIO18.
raw stuck near 8388607 or −8388608A load cell wire is open, or E+/E− is on A+/A−. Re-check with the meter.
Reading moves the wrong wayFlip LOAD_SIGN (1 or -1) in the sketch, or swap white and green at the HX711.
Press does not stop at the limitReading goes negative under load (flip LOAD_SIGN), or the scale is uncalibrated so the screen value is lower than the real force.
Jumps by kilos with nothing on itLoose wire, long untwisted leads near the 12 V wiring, or no shared ground.
Slowly drifts after power-upWarm-up. Wait a minute, then press t.
Reads about two-thirds of the real weightNot calibrated yet at 3.3 V. Do step 4.
