AUTOBRIQ · bench test

Wiring and testing the water solenoid valve

Relay 6 (GPIO32) switches 12 V to the valve. The firmware already has it: WATER ON (R6) on the Nextion and phone, key 6 on serial, and step 1 of AUTO RUN. No reflash needed.

Relay map note

The relay channels in this guide are the bench rig as wired (CH5 mixer, CH6 water valve). The thesis assigns CH5 to the water valve and CH6 to the 30 A automotive relay for the mixer. See docs/PROJECT_CONTEXT.md in the repository. Swap two signal wires and two GPIO entries before the final build if the thesis map is kept.

Read the valve's label before wiring

This guide assumes 12 V DC, normally closed (NC). A 24 V or AC valve needs its own supply on the relay contacts, like the mixer. Also note its minimum pressure: "0 MPa" opens from a bucket, while "0.02 MPa" or more needs tap pressure. Wire everything with the EcoFlow off.

Most valve coils have no polarity. The diode gives them one: whichever lead you call + gets the stripe and the wire from COM. Use 18 AWG or thicker; a typical 12 V valve draws 0.3 to 0.6 A.

Plumbing

Water path

Water supply (tap or raised bucket) → valve IN (follow the arrow on the valve body) → YF-S201 (its arrow the same way) → outlet into the mixing container. The flow sensor after the valve only counts while the valve is open, so the litres match what the valve let through.

Test

Dry first, then with water

  1. Meter the coil before power

    • EcoFlow off. Meter on ohms across valve + and −.
    Pass: about 20 to 40 Ω for a 12 V valve. Near 0 Ω means the diode is backwards or there's a short: fix it before powering.

  2. Dry click test

    • EcoFlow on, E-stop released, no water connected.
    • Tap WATER ON (R6), or press 6 on serial. Tap again to stop.
    Pass: relay 6 clicks and its LED lights, the valve gives a solid clunk on open and again on close. Meter across valve + and − reads about 12 V when on, 0 V when off.

  3. E-stop check

    • Tap WATER ON, then press the E-stop.
    Pass: the valve clunks shut even though relay 6 is still on. Release the E-stop and tap STOP ALL.

  4. Wet test

    • Connect the water path. Tap WATER ON with the outlet over a container.
    • Watch the FLOW box on the Nextion climb. Tap again to stop.
    Pass: water flows only while WATER ON is green, FLOW climbs while it flows, and no drip when closed.

  5. Dose in AUTO RUN

    • Tap AUTO RUN. Step 1 is water.
    Pass: the valve closes by itself at 1.0 L on the FLOW count (or after 60 s if the flow is weak), then the mixer starts. Change the dose with WATER_TARGET_L in the sketch.

Every water run stops by itself after 70 s. STOP ALL, the E-stop or any tap closes the valve right away.

Troubleshooting

When something's off

SymptomLikely cause
Relay clicks, valve silentNo 12 V at NO: E-stop pressed, or the wire is on NC. Meter COM to GND with WATER ON: should read 12 V.
Valve clunks but no water comesPressure below the valve's minimum (a pilot valve on a bucket), or the valve is in backwards. Check the arrow and the label's MPa.
Water flows the moment power is onValve on relay NC, or it is a normally open (NO) valve.
Drips when closedInstalled backwards, or grit on the seal. Flush it.
ESP32 or Nextion resets when the valve closesDiode missing. Fit the 1N4007 right at the valve terminals.
EcoFlow or E-stop line trips as soon as the valve opensDiode backwards: it shorts 12 V. Turn it around, stripe to valve +.
Valve gets hotNormal for long runs. Above 70 °C to the touch, check the voltage matches the label.
