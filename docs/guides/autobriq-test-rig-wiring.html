Wiring the actuators and mixer to the ESP32

For the actuator-test sketch (branch test/actuator-bench-mixer). It covers the 15 cm trapdoor actuator, the 40 cm press actuator, the mixer motor on relay 5, the 8-channel relay board, the ESP32 and the Nextion. It also covers the water solenoid on relay 6 and the YF-S201 flow sensor. The load cell has its own guide. This page describes the test rig (5 V from a power module); the final build with USB-A 5 V, bus terminals, a 10 A fuse and a perfboard is in the Perfboard Layout guide.

Relay map note

The relay channels in this guide are the bench rig as wired (CH5 mixer, CH6 water valve). The thesis assigns CH5 to the water valve and CH6 to the 30 A automotive relay for the mixer. See docs/PROJECT_CONTEXT.md in the repository. Swap two signal wires and two GPIO entries before the final build if the thesis map is kept.

Before you touch a wire

Do all wiring with the EcoFlow off and the AC-DC supply unplugged from the wall. Keep the E-stop within reach whenever switched 12 V is live. Leave the actuators and the mixer unplugged until their meter checks pass. One wrong H-bridge wire shorts 12 V straight to ground.

Part 1

Control side: ESP32, relay board, Nextion

Every signal wire runs straight across. Power pins are marked with a +5V or GND tag: connect them to the 5 V bus and the common ground block from Part 2.

Every +5V tag is the same wire: the 5 V power module's output. ESP32 VIN, the Nextion's red wire and relay JD-VCC each connect to it in parallel. The ESP32 does not power the Nextion: the 7" panel pulls up to about 500 mA, more than a USB port can supply together with the ESP32's Wi-Fi.

The 5 V power module

Turns the 12 V supply into a steady 5 V for the control side. Rate it 1.5 A or more: the 7" Nextion takes about 500 mA, and the ESP32's Wi-Fi bursts add up to 500 mA. A small breadboard module (MB102 type, about 700 mA) is borderline. An adjustable buck board such as the LM2596 also works: it has a brass screw (trimpot) to set the output. Either way, meter the output at 5.0 V before connecting anything.

Wi-Fi needs the power module

On laptop USB alone (about 500 mA) the ESP32 dies about 2 s after Wi-Fi starts: serial goes dead, the Nextion falls back to its demo, taps do nothing. With ESP32 VIN, the Nextion's red wire and JD-VCC on the 5 V power module and one shared ground, it stays up. If you ever have to run on USB alone, set WEB_ENABLED = false in the sketch and re-flash.

Part 2

Power side: 12 V, E-stop and the two H-bridges

Each actuator uses two relays. Both NO contacts go to switched +12 V, both NC contacts go to ground, and the two COM contacts go to the two actuator leads. When both relays are off, both leads sit at ground, so the actuator is braked.

Part 3

Mixer: AC-DC supply, relay 5 and the motor driver

The mixer runs from its own AC-DC supply, not from the EcoFlow 12 V. Relay CH5 is a plain switch in the supply's V+ line: COM takes PSU V+, NO feeds the motor driver's V+ input, NC stays empty. Tap MIXER ON on the Nextion and GPIO23 goes LOW, CH5 closes, and the driver turns the motor.

AC mains (L, N, PE) PSU V+ (14 AWG) PSU V− (14 AWG) motor lead relay signal
Mains on this side

The PSU's L, N and PE terminals carry wall voltage. Use a fused, earthed plug cord, put the terminal cover back on before you plug it in, and never work on it while it is plugged in. The E-stop does not cut this supply. To stop the mixer fast, tap STOP ALL or pull the PSU plug.

Part 4

Water: solenoid valve on relay 6, YF-S201 flow sensor

Relay CH6 switches the solenoid from switched +12 V, so the E-stop also shuts the water. The flow sensor runs on the 5 V power module, and its signal reaches GPIO13 through one 10k resistor, which keeps the 5 V pulse from pushing current into the 3.3 V pin. In AUTO RUN, the valve closes at 1.0 L (450 pulses per litre), or after 60 s if the flow is too low.

switched +12 V ground +5 V (power module) flow signal
Check the valve's label first

This assumes a 12 V DC, normally closed valve. A 24 V or AC valve needs its own supply on CH6 COM/NO, like the mixer in Part 3. Mount the flow sensor with its arrow pointing the way the water runs. It reads low or nothing if it's backwards.

Bring-up

Test in this order

Don't move to the next step until the current one passes.

  1. Power only

    • EcoFlow timeouts set to Never in the app.
    • Meter the 5 V power module's output with nothing connected to it.
    Pass: meter reads 5.0 V. Pressing the E-stop kills switched 12 V but the 5 V stays on.

  2. ESP32 + relay board, no 12 V on the contacts

    • Remove the blue jumper. VCC to ESP32 3V3, JD-VCC to +5 V, GND to ground.
    • Flash: pio run -e actuator-test -t upload, then tap each button.
    Pass: CH1 to CH5 each click and light their LED. Nothing clicks while the ESP32 powers up or when you press EN (reset).

  3. H-bridges with the actuators unplugged

    • Wire NO, NC and COM as in Part 2. Turn on switched 12 V.
    • Put the meter across COM1 and COM2, then across COM3 and COM4.
    Pass: 0 V with all relays off, about 12 V when a channel is on, and the sign flips between the two buttons of a pair. No spark, no hot wire.

  4. Actuators connected

    • Plug in the 15 cm actuator, try CLOSE then OPEN. Then the 40 cm, PRESS then HOME.
    • If a button moves the wrong way, swap that actuator's two leads at COM. No code change.
    Pass: each one moves the right way and stops by itself at its built-in end limit.

  5. Mixer, motor unplugged first

    • Wire Part 3 with the PSU unplugged. Leave the motor off the driver's M+ and M−.
    • Plug in the PSU. Meter on the driver's V+ in and V− in, then tap MIXER ON and tap it again.
    • Connect the motor and repeat. If it turns the wrong way, swap M+ and M−.
    Pass: 0 V at the driver with MIXER off, full PSU voltage with it on. The motor spins on MIXER ON and stops on a second tap or STOP ALL.

  6. Water: valve, then flow sensor

    • Wire Part 4. With the hose off, tap WATER ON: the valve clicks open, tap again and it clicks shut.
    • Connect the water. Tap WATER ON with a measuring jug under the outlet and stop at 1 L on the jug.
    • Compare with the litres on the status line. If they differ, set PULSES_PER_L to 450 × screen litres ÷ jug litres.
    Pass: the litres count climbs while water runs and matches the jug within about 5%. AUTO RUN closes the valve at 1.0 L.
Running the test

Three ways to drive it

Nextion

Tap a button to start that move, the mixer or the water; it turns green. While water runs, the status line shows the litres so far. Tap it again, or the red STOP ALL bar, to stop. The blue AUTO RUN button plays the sequence below.

Phone or laptop

Join Actuator-Test, password OperatorPassword, open http://192.168.4.1. Same buttons, AUTO RUN included.

Serial Monitor

At 115200 baud, press 1 to 4 for the four moves, 5 for the mixer, 6 for water, a for AUTO RUN, any other key to stop.

AUTO RUN sequence

One relay at a time, 100 ms gap between steps, about 90 seconds plus the water fill. Any tap, any key or STOP ALL ends it with all relays off. Tapping AUTO RUN again starts over from step 1, so jog the trapdoor and press home by hand first if a run was stopped halfway. The times live in AUTO[] near the top of actuator_test.cpp.

Every move, mixer included, stops by itself after 70 s. A full 40 cm stroke takes about 57 s. Only one relay is ever on, so starting the mixer stops any actuator and the other way round, with 100 ms off-time before the change. If the phone drops off Wi-Fi mid-move, the actuator keeps going to its end limit, so use the E-stop for a fast stop.

Troubleshooting

When something's off

SymptomLikely cause
No relay clicks at allJD-VCC has no 5 V, or the blue jumper is still on while VCC is on 3V3.
All relays on and won't turn offRelay VCC is on 5 V instead of 3V3. A 3.3 V HIGH can't switch the opto off then.
Press twitches at bootIN4 still wired to GPIO14. Move it to GPIO33.
Nextion blank or ignores tapsTX and RX swapped, or no shared ground. The Nextion's blue wire goes to GPIO16.
Nextion shows the factory demo, taps do nothing, STOP ALL missingThe ESP32 died after boot. If it happens about 2 s in, Wi-Fi start-up current is browning it out: keep WEB_ENABLED = false or power the ESP32 and Nextion from the 5 V power module.
Nextion has no MIXER or AUTO RUN buttonOld sketch on the ESP32. Flash the test/actuator-bench-mixer build.
Upload fails: "The chip stopped responding"Close any serial monitor on the port, replug USB, hold BOOT until "Connecting..." shows. A failed upload leaves the ESP32 boot-looping until a good one lands.
Mixer runs as soon as the PSU is plugged inPSU V+ is on CH5 NC instead of NO, or CH5 is bypassed.
Water runs but litres stay at 0.00Sensor not on 5 V, yellow wire not reaching GPIO13, or the sensor is fitted backwards. If the yellow wire shows no pulses on a meter, add a 10k from yellow to +5 V.
AUTO RUN says "time cap hit" on waterFlow too low to reach 1.0 L in 60 s: low water pressure, or the valve needs more pressure to open fully. Raise the cap or lower WATER_TARGET_L.
ESP32 resets when the valve closesFlyback diode missing or backwards across the solenoid coil.
CH5 clicks but the mixer doesn't turnDriver's V− not on PSU V−, driver enable or speed knob at zero, or a loose motor lead.
ESP32 or Nextion resets when an actuator starts5 V sags on motor inrush. Check that the 5 V power module is fed from unswitched 12 V with thick wire, and that grounds meet at one block.
Actuator hums but doesn't moveLead on NO and NC of the same relay, or a loose 14 AWG connection. Re-check Part 2.
